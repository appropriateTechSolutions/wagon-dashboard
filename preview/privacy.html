Privacy Policy — Wagon Inspector

Last updated 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Wagon Inspector Android app and its companion web dashboard (the Wagon Structural Dashboard) handle, why, and what choices you have. The app is a work tool for rail-wagon structural inspection. It is provided by WQMS, which is responsible for your information (the data controller).

Contact: apps@wqms.com.au

Who can use the app

The app is only for people who have been given an account by WQMS or by an administrator of their organisation. There is no public sign-up, no public content and nothing to buy. It is not directed at children.

Information we handle

  • Account details — your email address and password (used to sign in; passwords are handled by our authentication service and are never visible to WQMS), your first and last name, an optional phone number, the organisation you belong to, your role and the sites you can access. You can edit your own name and phone number in the app.
  • Inspection records — the wagons inspected, defects and measurements recorded, notes, dispositions, areas marked as not inspected, and the date, time and person for each entry. Changes are kept in an audit trail (who changed what, and when).
  • Photos — photos you take with the device camera, or choose from the device, and attach to an inspection or defect. The app only receives the photos you pick; it does not browse your photo library. Photos can contain information the camera adds itself (such as the time taken, and a location if location tagging is turned on in your camera settings); the app does not read or use location.
  • Sign-off signatures — when an inspection is signed off, the signature drawn on screen is saved as an image together with the signer's name and the time.
  • Names of field staff — administrators can keep a list of names (and optionally email addresses) of staff who use a shared tablet, so inspections and sign-offs can be attributed to the right person.
  • Support requests — anything you write in Support & feedback, and the administrator's reply.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, call logs, messages, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

How the information is used

  • To sign you in and show you only the organisations, sites and wagons you are allowed to see.
  • To record, review, report on and sign off wagon inspections — the purpose of the app. Reports (PDF) are created on the device and only shared when you choose to print or share them.
  • To send account emails you or your administrator ask for, such as an invitation or a password-reset link.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Storage on your device and syncing

So the app keeps working without a signal, it stores a working copy of your inspection data, the photos and signatures you capture, cached reference images and your sign-in session in the app's private storage on the device. Changes are uploaded to the server automatically when a connection is available. Signing out ends the session on that device; uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Information is stored with Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), on cloud infrastructure in the Sydney, Australia region. Account emails are delivered through our email service providers. These providers process the information only to run the service for WQMS.

Inside the service, what you record is visible to the people who work on the same organisation's inspections (according to their role and site access) and to WQMS administrators who support the service. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

All communication between the app and the server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting information

Inspection records are kept for as long as your organisation uses the service, because they form the maintenance history of the wagons. Accounts are kept while they are in use.

To delete your account, or to ask for a copy, a correction or the deletion of other information about you, email apps@wqms.com.au from the email address on your account (or ask your organisation's administrator). We will deactivate the account straight away and remove your personal details within 30 days. Inspection records and sign-offs you made may be kept in your organisation's records where they are needed for safety and maintenance history; in that case they are kept only for that purpose.

Security

Access is by individual sign-in, and every request is checked on the server against your organisation, role and sites. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
